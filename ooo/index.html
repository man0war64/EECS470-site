What to watch when it runs

    Quiz: fill in every structure as it looks at the end of each cycle.
    Write tags as on the slides: p5, or p5+ when ready. Head/tail column: h, t, or ht. Busy column: y or blank. Empty cells stay blank (or -). Free list is in order, oldest first, comma or space separated. Tab moves between cells. Your answers are saved in this browser as you type. The timing table fills itself in from your ROB answers: D when an instruction first appears in the ROB, S/X/C from its ROB row, R when it has left; the pipeline diagram is drawn from it. Cycles before the start cycle are given: shown worked out, with their event log, for you to study but not to fill in. Drag & drop is on: drag an instruction from the program table into a ROB slot (it arrives as the whole expression, graded the same as I0), and from a ROB slot into a reservation station's op cell (it arrives as just I0).
