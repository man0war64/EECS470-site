Quiz: fill in every structure as it looks at the end of each cycle.
Write tags as on the slides: p5, or p5+ when ready. Head/tail column: h, t, or ht. Busy column: y or blank. Empty cells stay blank (or -). Free list is in order, oldest first, comma or space separated. Every cell takes a number: an instruction is its number in the program (I3 is 3), a physical register its number (p5 is 5), and S, X and C the cycle. Tick rdy beside a register that holds its value, and busy for a station in use; choose h, t or ht for the head and tail of the ROB. Empty cells stay blank. The free list is in order, oldest first, from the first slot on; the CDB has a field for each tag it can carry in a cycle. A number this machine or this program does not have (a register past its last, a cycle that has not happened yet) is outlined in red and is not saved. Tab moves between cells. Your answers are saved in this browser as you type. The timing table fills itself in from your ROB answers: D when an instruction first appears in the ROB, S/X/C from its ROB row, R when it has left; the pipeline diagram is drawn from it. A given cycle (before the start cycle, or a homework problem's checkpoint between the cycles to fill in) is shown worked out, with its event log, for you to study but not to fill in. Drag & drop is on: drag an instruction from the program (⊞ panels: put the machine and the program beside the tables) into a ROB slot (it arrives as the whole expression, graded the same as I0), and from a ROB slot into a reservation station's op cell (it arrives as just I0) (it arrives as its number).
