Questions to Ponder

161 questions from the lectures, gathered in one place and sorted by what they are about.

These are not homework. They are the questions worth being able to answer — in a lecture, in an exam, or in an interview. If one of them stops you, that is the useful signal: go back to the lecture, the reading, or ask on Piazza.

Several appear more than once across the term. That is deliberate on the slides and has been collapsed here, so each question is listed once, under the topic it belongs to.
